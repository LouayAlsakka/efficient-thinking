# Preference statements — the personal arm of Efficient Thinking IV

The persistence experiment (E5) asks whether a specific person's stated taste transfers into a model, in context
or by a small fine-tune, as judged by a frontier model. It needs one thing from that person: about twenty short
statements of preference, written once, in their own words, in any language. No rating sessions, no reading of
model output. The two letters below are the same request in English and Chinese; either alone is enough.

Rules kept: the person is pseudonymous in the paper (the reviewer/experimenter convention of Paper VIII); the
statements are stored under this directory as `<pseudonym>.md` and never quoted with a name; nothing is paraphrased
before it is used — the statements are the instrument, verbatim.
