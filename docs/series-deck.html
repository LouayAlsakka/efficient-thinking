The claim, the method, and where in the chain it works

What the series says, in the sentences the papers use.
8
The claim. When intelligence is held fixed, the value of experience depends strongly on how that experience was acquired, not merely on how much experience is stored or how it is retrieved. Every clause is a measured result: acquisition (VIII-c), amount (VIII-b), retrieval (VIII-d, VIII-e).
The method. The model's parameters are its thinking and are never changed; everything else that can move search — what the model is given to read, where that experience is collected, how it is applied at a decision — is varied one at a time and measured. Holding the thinking fixed is what lets each gain be attributed to experience.
Where it works. The head decides where the next inspection goes, which is where the next unit of computation goes: a learned search prior, closer to a tree search's selection rule than to an action classifier. Its measured effect is on allocation: equal quality at 2.32× less compute.
acquisition policy→experience→search policy→computation→outcomewith the intelligence fixed at every link

What the method risks, and how that is measured

Changing parameters carries a cost nobody can quantify. This series never changes one, so that cost is zero by construction. Its own risk is stated and measured.
9

The risk. The head chooses among candidates the model itself proposed, so it can steer search away from a place where the vanilla model would have succeeded.

The measurement. Problem by problem, on the same problems: the base-only cell of a paired four-cell table (both solve · head only · base only · neither), published beside every headline, with both denominators.

Across the closed arms: 1.2% to 12.2% of the task set; 11.7% to 59.8% of the problems the vanilla model itself solved. The regression does not track the gain: the largest gains carry the smallest regressions. A head is applied only within the family it was fitted on; outside it the vanilla model runs unchanged.

  • The second denominator is the one that prices the behaviour
  • A head outside its family: registered as the next control
